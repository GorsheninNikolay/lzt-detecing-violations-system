- (direction) Headless express distillation selected; unresolved design inputs remain open questions.
- (decision) The spec slug is construction-monitoring-concepts, inherited from the forged-idea parent folder.
- (decision) The rapid MVP focuses on excavation-pit soil removal and helps a site manager inspect potential delay rather than declaring a violation.
- (capability) CAP-1: Analyze one image of the controlled excavation area for excavator and dump-truck observations; success is one explicit observation state per class tied to that image.
- (capability) CAP-2: Analyze an ordered image series from one controlled area and period; success is an aggregate observation with the period and evidence images, or insufficient-data when the series cannot support evaluation.
- (capability) CAP-3: Represent each requested analysis with one of detected, not detected in frame, insufficient data, or not analyzed; success is deterministic use of the four defined meanings without treating in-frame absence as site-wide absence.
- (capability) CAP-4: Evaluate observations against an explicit work rule while separating observation, expectation, and recommended check; success is a traceable warning that exposes all three and the rule provenance.
- (capability) CAP-5: Demonstrate the excavation rule where an excavator is continuously expected and a dump truck periodically expected; success is a positive case with both classes and no haulage-delay warning plus a negative series with persistent dump-truck non-detection and a request to check a possible delay.
- (capability) CAP-6: Produce prototype-readiness evidence on 10 to 15 images against manual labels; success is recorded misses, false detections, repeat-result stability, and the outcome of both demonstration cases.
- (constraint) The system must not determine a legal or contractual violation or take action for the manager.
- (constraint) A single image may support only detected or not detected in frame, never an assertion that equipment is absent from the whole site.
- (constraint) Series-based warnings apply only to images from the same controlled area over a defined observation period.
- (constraint) The MVP analyzes only excavation-pit soil removal and the excavator and dump-truck classes; work not observable through these classes is not analyzed.
- (constraint) Every rule must identify its provenance as project document, normative or methodological source, expert heuristic, or demonstration rule; heuristics must not appear as normative requirements.
- (constraint) Cloud, local, or hybrid execution remains undecided until comparative evidence is available; the product contract must not prescribe a technology.
- (decision) State semantics, the demonstration rule and cases, and prototype readiness criteria are load-bearing catalogs and belong in prototype-scenarios.md.
- (assumption) The MVP receives the excavation-pit scenario and its observation period explicitly; deriving the current stage from a dated construction schedule is outside this prototype.
- (question) What minimum number, cadence, and duration of usable images makes a series sufficient for the periodic dump-truck rule?
- (question) Which visibility, framing, and image-quality conditions make an input insufficient data instead of not detected in frame?
- (question) What acceptance thresholds for misses, false detections, and repeat-result stability are required beyond passing the two demonstration cases?
- (decision) Explicit non-goals are legal verdicts, automated management action, site-wide absence claims from one frame, all eight equipment classes, exhaustive mapping of the work catalog, and premature execution-technology selection.
- (note) Wrapper-only content: the request to invoke bmad-spec and its formatting wrapper are process instructions, not product contract content.
- (constraint) A rule links work type to an observable process and classifies each equipment expectation as continuously expected, periodically expected, or not informative.
- (constraint) Prototype readiness includes recording whether a reviewer can understand each warning's reason and recommended check.
