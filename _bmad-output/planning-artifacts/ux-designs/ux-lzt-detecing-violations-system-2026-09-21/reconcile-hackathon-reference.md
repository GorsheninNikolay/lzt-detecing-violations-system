# Hackathon Task Page Reference Reconciliation

Source: [hackathon-task-page-reference.png](imports/hackathon-task-page-reference.png)

The reference informs visual lineage only. `DESIGN.md` and `EXPERIENCE.md` remain authoritative if this image conflicts with the product contracts.

## Retained

- Dark plum and near-black violet surfaces establish continuity with the hackathon identity.
- Violet remains the brand and interaction accent.
- Bright, high-contrast typography and subtle panel boundaries support the selected full-night presentation.
- Generous negative space and restrained decoration inform the product's visual tone.

## Transformed

- The reference's dark shell becomes a full-night product theme across stage overview, analysis flow, evidence, history, and readiness surfaces.
- Editorial cards become compact, task-oriented product panels with clearer hierarchy, scanability, responsive behavior, and accessible interaction states.
- Large display typography is reduced to product-appropriate headings; dense operational content uses Onest at readable interface sizes.
- Violet identifies brand, focus, selection, and primary actions only. Semantic status colors stay independent: amber for `Requires check`, green for successful completion, and red for technical errors.

## Dropped

- Hackathon-site navigation, menu utilities, search, social links, and account controls.
- The task number and its oversized decorative treatment.
- The authorization call to action.
- Long editorial and promotional content blocks, prize information, organizer branding, and language controls.
- Any event-page status meaning or decorative color usage that could be mistaken for product state.

The resulting interface should feel recognizably related to the hackathon page without copying its marketing layout or weakening the product's information hierarchy, accessibility, or evidence-bound status semantics.
