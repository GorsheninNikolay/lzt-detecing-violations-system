# Dataset landscape digest — round 1

- **AIDCON:** Official AI2LAB card reports 2,155 UAV images and 9,563 machine instances, including `Dump truck` and `excavator`; official 2024 paper documents COCO-style annotations with `bbox` and segmentation and an 80/20 experiment split. Access is research-only CC BY-NC 4.0, therefore not commercial training-ready. Sources: https://www.ai2lab.org/aidcon/ and https://www.mdpi.com/2072-4292/16/17/3295. Confidence: high.
- **CMOT:** Official repository states 100 videos, 77k+ annotated frames, 155k instances, explicit dump-truck/excavator classes, and CC BY-NC 4.0. Its official visualizer confirms frame/track/bbox/class annotations and class mappings. It is a strong source for a technical prototype but not commercial training; detector split must be by video. Sources: https://github.com/XZ-YAN/CMOT-Dataset and https://github.com/XZ-YAN/CMOT-Dataset/blob/main/CMOT_annotations_visualizer.py. Confidence: high.
- **Liebherr Product:** Official card reports 15k+ images across 23 machine classes, including articulated dump trucks and excavators. It is gated, non-commercial, and uses pseudo-labels; detector-ready splits and class totals are not established. Sources: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Moonxc/Liebherr_Product, https://github.com/chen-xin-94/DART, https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.09174. Confidence: medium-high.
- **Conclusion:** No strict-shortlist candidate has commercial training rights. Request AIDCON and use CMOT only for research/prototype experiments; commercial route remains open.
