# Annotation and RF-DETR fit digest — round 1

- **Excavator Detector:** Fixed Hugging Face mirror has 2,656 images, explicit `excavators`, `dump truck`, `wheel loader` taxonomy, COCO boxes, and train/valid/test split 2,245/267/144; declared CC BY 4.0. It is the fastest technical bootstrap but needs source-rightsholder confirmation and a fixed revision/hash. Sources: https://huggingface.co/datasets/keremberke/excavator-detector/blob/main/README.md and https://universe.roboflow.com/mohamed-sabek-6zmr6/excavators-cwlh0. Confidence: high access/taxonomy; medium label quality/provenance.
- **CMOT:** Real construction video with MOTChallenge-like boxes and explicit class mappings; convert sampled video-separated frames to COCO. Use only after commercial permission. Sources: https://github.com/XZ-YAN/CMOT-Dataset and https://github.com/XZ-YAN/CMOT-Dataset/blob/main/CMOT_annotations_visualizer.py. Confidence: high technical, low commercial certainty.
- **ACID:** Strong semantic fit but research-only/gated and commercially ambiguous; not a rapid source. Source: https://www.acidb.net/dataset. Confidence: high.
- **Open Images V7:** Does not have excavator/dump-truck box classes, only generic truck; it cannot train the required two-class detector. Sources: https://storage.googleapis.com/openimages/web/download_v7.html and https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics/blob/main/ultralytics/cfg/datasets/open-images-v7.yaml. Confidence: high.
- **Guardrails:** Normalize labels to `excavator`/`dump_truck`, split by video/site/source before randomization, manually QA 100 boxes/source, and treat target-camera transfer as unproven.
