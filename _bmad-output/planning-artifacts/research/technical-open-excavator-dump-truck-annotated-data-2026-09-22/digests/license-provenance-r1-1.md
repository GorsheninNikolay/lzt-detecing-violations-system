# Licence and provenance digest — round 1

- **SynDAB:** Fraunhofer/University dataset is CC BY 4.0 and synthetic, so potentially commercially usable with attribution; it is excavator-viewpoint/barrel-focused and not a two-class detector corpus. Source: https://darus.uni-stuttgart.de/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=doi:10.18419/darus-3758. Confidence: high licence, medium fit.
- **Mohamed Sabek Roboflow Excavators:** Card declares CC BY 4.0, 2,655 images, excavator and dump-truck classes with boxes. It is commercially compatible on its face but has no primary-image chain of title; use needs written warranty/confirmation from uploader. Source: https://universe.roboflow.com/mohamed-sabek-6zmr6/excavators-cwlh0. Confidence: medium.
- **CMOT/AIDCON/LP:** Valuable technically but CC BY-NC/research-only, hence excluded from commercial training absent written grant. Sources: https://github.com/XZ-YAN/CMOT-Dataset, https://www.ai2lab.org/aidcon/, https://huggingface.co/datasets/Moonxc/Liebherr_Product. Confidence: high.
- **ACID, EXC Video, ExtCon:** Exclude: direct non-commercial restriction or absent dataset/weight licence. Sources: https://www.acidb.net/dataset, https://huggingface.co/datasets/fuxi-robot/excavator-video, https://github.com/dyxm/ExtCon. Confidence: high.
- **Licence rule:** CC BY permits commercial adaptation only to the rights actually held by the licensor; CC0 cannot waive third-party rights; CC BY-NC-SA is non-commercial. Preserve source/version/licence/creator/retrieval manifests and seek written grant covering ML training, commercial deployment, and derived weights. Sources: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/legalcode.
